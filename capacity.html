Capacity Planning

Strategic Capacity Planning represents the critical first step in operations management. It establishes the overall level of productive resources required to meet long-term anticipated demand. By carefully calculating design capacity, effective capacity, and system utilization, organizations can determine the optimal scale of their facilities, preventing costly bottlenecks, underutilized capital, and lost market share.

Design Capacity

The maximum theoretical output of a system under ideal conditions. It assumes continuous operation with no interruptions.

Effective Capacity

The expected output given realistic operating constraints. It accounts for planned maintenance, setup times, and breaks.

Actual Output

The rate of output actually achieved. It cannot exceed effective capacity and often suffers from unplanned downtime or defects.

Key Metrics

Utilization: Actual Output / Design Capacity
Efficiency: Actual Output / Effective Capacity

Operational Inputs

1. Baseline Design Parameters

2. Capacity Loss Factors (OEE Model)
