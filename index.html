Welcome to OM Professor

This interactive learning application is based on William J. Stevenson - Operations Management Ed 14. Explore concepts, solve dynamic problems, and evaluate your understanding of core OM principles.

Design vs. Effective capacity, utilization, and bottleneck analysis.
