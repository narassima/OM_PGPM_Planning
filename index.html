Operations Planner Workspace

Welcome to the central command hub for operations management. This comprehensive planning environment bridges the gap between high-level business strategy and granular factory-floor execution. Use these interconnected modules to model real-world scenarios: establish your theoretical and effective Capacity limits, define your optimal Aggregate Production Plan (APP) to balance workforce and inventory costs against fluctuating demand, disaggregate those plans into a precise Master Production Schedule (MPS), and finally drive the exact timing of sub-assemblies and raw materials via the Material Requirements Planning (MRP) engine using the exact structure of your Bill of Materials (BOM).

Learning Modules

Capacity Planning

Design vs. Effective capacity, utilization, and bottleneck analysis.

Aggregate Planning

Chase vs. Level strategies, demand matching, and cost computations.

Master Prod. Schedule

Disaggregation and Available-To-Promise (ATP) inventory calculations.

MRP

Gross requirements, netting, and planned order releases.

Bill of Materials

Product structure trees and low-level coding.
