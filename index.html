How to Navigate

Welcome to the Production Planner. Here is how to get the most out of it:

  • Sidebar: All modules are always accessible on the left.
  • Unified Planner: Use this to see how a change in BOM cost affects the total MRP spend in one screen.
  • Interactive Tools: Use these for "what-if" scenarios like EOQ or OEE simulations.
  • Export: Use the "Download" button at the top right of any page to save your work as PDF or Excel.
  • Resource Bank: Access curated papers, cases, and journals for your studies.
